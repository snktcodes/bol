Privacy Policy

Bol doesn't collect, sell or use any data about you. This page explains where your information goes when you use the app and this website, because some of it goes to services you choose.

The app collects nothing

  • Bol has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and we run no servers that the app talks to.
  • Everything Bol keeps stays on your Mac in ~/.bol: your conversations (threads), tasks, memory, a week of dictations, logs and settings.
  • API keys you add for OpenAI or Noiz AI are stored only on your Mac, in ~/.bol/config.json. They're sent only to the provider they belong to. When you agree to the Terms in setup, Bol records which version you agreed to and when, in the same file; that isn't sent anywhere either.
  • The only request the app makes to us is checking agentbol.com/latest.json for a new version. It sends nothing about you beyond what any web request carries (your IP address and a standard request), and we don't log or use it.

Services you choose

Bol works by handing what you say to services you already use, with your own accounts and keys. What they do with it is governed by their own policies, not this one.

  • Anthropic (Claude). Your requests, and your voice when Claude transcribes it, go to Anthropic through the Claude Code on your Mac and your own Claude account, just as they would when you use Claude Code yourself. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI and Noiz AI. Only if you choose them: text Bol speaks, or audio it transcribes, goes to that provider with your API key, under its own privacy policy.
  • Tools you connect. MCP servers, Claude in Chrome and the apps Bol controls on your behalf receive whatever a request needs them to.

This website

agentbol.com is hosted on Cloudflare. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate without cookies and without tracking you across sites. Cloudflare processes requests (including IP addresses) to serve and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The site sets no cookies of its own and has no forms. Its fonts are served from agentbol.com itself.

Email

If you email support@agentbol.com, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Children

Bol is for adults: you must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live. It also requires a Claude subscription, which has its own age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date, and when a change needs your agreement, Bol asks again in the app. Since the app collects nothing, a change would mostly concern the website or a new optional service.

Contact

Bol is made by snktcodes, based on the original by Mehul Mohan. Questions about privacy: support@agentbol.com.
